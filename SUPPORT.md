# Getting help

1. **Check the built-in help.** *VPN > WG Suite > Help* is searchable and covers
   every page, option and action.
2. **Run the Connectivity doctor** on the peer (stethoscope on its row). Most
   connection problems show up there with a fix.
3. **Look at the Audit page.** *Installation health* at the top lists anything
   wrong with the install, and *Recently refused* lists requests WG Suite
   deliberately did not act on.
4. **Search the [issues](https://github.com/3um3le3ee/pfSense-wireguard-peer-export/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**,
   open and closed.
5. **Ask.** Open a [question or bug report](https://github.com/3um3le3ee/pfSense-wireguard-peer-export/issues/new/choose),
   or post in the WG Suite thread on the Netgate forum.

WG Suite is a free community project with no paid support. It is not supported
by Netgate, so please do not ask Netgate support about it.
