# Code of conduct

WG Suite is a small community project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or the Netgate forum thread, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to pfSense or
  WireGuard
- keep criticism about the code and the behaviour, not the person
- assume good faith and ask before concluding someone meant harm
- respect other people's privacy: never post someone else's keys, addresses or
  personal details

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
threats, and publishing anyone's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts
that break these rules. To report a problem privately, email
**3um3le3ee@mail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
